ROLEBOARD — LOCAL JOB APPLICATION TRACKER

Double-click Start Roleboard.cmd to launch the local website. Keep its terminal
window open while using Roleboard. No installation is required.

Your checkbox, stage, dates, and notes are saved by the browser on this device.
The opportunity data is stored separately in jobs.js so automated updates do not
overwrite your application progress.

Filters:
- Search by company, role, location, or JD text
- Filter by internship type or application stage
- Show urgent roles only
- See a 0–100% resume-match score and the reason for it on every role

Thresholds:
- Internship: at least ₹30,000 per month
- Full-time: at least ₹8 LPA
- Eligibility: May 2027 CS graduate in India
